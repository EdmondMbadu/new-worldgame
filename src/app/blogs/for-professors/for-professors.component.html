In the classroom

Drexel University, ENTP 601: Social and Sustainable Innovation

In Summer 2026, graduate students in Drexel’s entrepreneurship program ran their team projects in a single Global Solutions Lab class space, with the syllabus, discussion and meeting room in one place.

Most teams chose problems in their own city: the Philadelphia Food Recovery Network, Philadelphia Air Pollution, and the waste and recycling piling up on Philadelphia’s streets. Others took on access to wellness, youth wellbeing and climate resilience.

Where the method comes from

“Make the world work for 100% of humanity.”

The aim of Fuller’s World Game

In the 1960s, Buckminster Fuller proposed a “World Game”: a way for anyone to work out how to make the world work for everyone, using real data and design science instead of politics. Medard Gabel worked with Fuller and spent the next five decades turning that idea into workshops run with students, universities and organizations around the world.

Global Solutions Lab is the online version of that method, with AI colleagues added to give every team the kind of feedback a workshop facilitator would.
