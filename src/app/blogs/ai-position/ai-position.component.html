Our position on artificial intelligence

October 2026 · 4‑minute read

I. The moment

A fear is spreading through the world: that we have built something we cannot steer, and that it will end badly for us. We take that fear seriously, but we don’t accept that the ending is already decided.

Artificial intelligence is a tool of unusual power. Fire, the printing press and the electric grid were too, and what each became depended on the people who built it, governed it and used it. AI is no different.

Its outcome isn’t written yet. People are deciding it now, and that is where our position and our hope begin.

II. Our starting point

The Global Solutions Lab grows out of Buckminster Fuller’s World Game and its challenge:

“To make the world work for 100% of humanity, in the shortest possible time, through spontaneous cooperation, without ecological offense or the disadvantage of anyone.”

R. Buckminster Fuller · The World Game

We hold AI to the same standard.

How intelligent it is doesn’t matter much. What matters is whether it helps more people live with dignity, security and opportunity, on a planet that can sustain them.

IV. On fear

The risks are real.

AI can be misused. It can concentrate power, erode trust and displace work and meaning. Some systems may act in ways we don’t fully understand. Ignoring these risks would be reckless.

Fear that paralyzes is a risk of its own, though. It hands the decisions to whoever is least concerned with the common good.
