Solution-a-thon sponsorship

Help students turn urgent problems into deployable solutions.

Sponsors make the Solution-a-thon possible by funding student awards, access, hub operations, and the support needed to move the strongest ideas beyond a one-day event.

Learn how to become a sponsor arrow_forward

Sponsor outcomes

Student impact: awards, access, and follow-on support for high-potential teams.

Mission visibility: association with practical work on SDG-aligned local and global problems.

Talent connection: early access to students who can think across systems, evidence, AI, and implementation.

Sponsor categories

Ways to support the event.

Title sponsor Anchor the national event, student awards, and closing livestream.
Track sponsor Support one SDG challenge track and the students working on that problem space.
Regional sponsor Help a regional hub recruit, feed, support, and celebrate teams.
Community partner Bring a real problem brief and help teams understand the communities affected.

For sponsor conversations

What we should discuss.

  • Which problem spaces align with your organization’s mission.
  • How your support can underwrite student access, prize money, or hub operations.
  • What visibility, reporting, judging, recruiting, or community engagement would be appropriate.
  • How winning Solution Briefs can continue into follow-on mentorship or implementation support.
