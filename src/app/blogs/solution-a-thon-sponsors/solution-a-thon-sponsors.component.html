§ 01 · The opportunity

Back students working on real problems.

The Solution-a-thon asks high school and university teams to design practical responses to urgent SDG-aligned problems in one focused day.

Sponsorship helps remove barriers for students, supports local host sites, funds the prize pool, and creates a stronger path for promising Solution Briefs after the event.

If you need the full event format, schedule, divisions, judging rubric, and prize structure, review the main program page.

§ 02 · Sponsor roles

Several ways to support the sprint.

We can shape the role around your mission, geography, and the kind of student impact you want to underwrite.

Title sponsor

Anchor the national Solution-a-thon.

Support the event, student awards, closing livestream, and national communications.

  • High-visibility event association
  • Support across both student divisions
  • Program impact reporting

Track sponsor

Fund work on a specific challenge.

Align with one SDG track and the teams designing solutions in that problem space.

  • Challenge-track recognition
  • Problem-space alignment
  • Optional judges or mentors

Regional sponsor

Help a local hub host teams well.

Support recruiting, food, operations, promotion, and day-of coordination at a regional site.

  • Local student access
  • Hub operations support
  • Community visibility

Community partner

Bring a real-world problem brief.

Help students understand the people, systems, and implementation constraints behind a challenge.

  • Practical context for teams
  • Mission-aligned engagement
  • Follow-on mentorship potential
