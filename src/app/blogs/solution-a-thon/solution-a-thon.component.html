§ 01 · The brief

Not a hackathon. A solution sprint.

The Solution-a-thon is a one-day, hybrid competition where teams of 3-5 pick one of five UN SDG challenges and build a comprehensive solution in 8 hours.

You do not need to code. You will not be judged on prototype polish. You will be judged by a design-science test: have you accurately described the problem, and comprehensively designed a solution that can actually be deployed?

Each team gets access to the Global Solutions Lab platform and Bucky, our AI co-pilot. At 4:30 PM ET, teams submit. At 6:30 PM ET, prizes are awarded on the closing livestream.

Design science is the organized use of imagination and science to develop innovative and viable solutions to critical real-world problems.

§ 02 · Two divisions

Built for every level of student.

One division for high schoolers, one for university students. Same five tracks, same judging rubric, separate prize pools.

High School Division

Grades 9-12. Public, private, charter, homeschool.

Teams of 3-5. Built for the student whose first portfolio piece is the one they are about to make.

  • $500 per Track winner
  • Bucky AI co-pilot access
  • Eligible for Grand Prize, AI-Native, People's Choice

University Division

Undergrad. Graduate. Two-year, four-year, online.

Teams of 3-5. Mixed teams with high school members compete in this division.

  • $1,000 per Track winner
  • Solutions Fellow credential for winners
  • Eligible for Grand Prize, AI-Native, People's Choice

Six criteria. Same rubric every team.

20%Near-term real-world impact within a 2-3 year horizon

20%Comprehensive anticipatory design
15%Feasibility and first-year execution
15%AI-native thinking and methodology
