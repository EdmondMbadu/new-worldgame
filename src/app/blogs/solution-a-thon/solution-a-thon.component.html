§ 01 · The brief

Not a hackathon. A solution sprint.

The Solution-a-thon is a half-day, hybrid competition where a community host determines one or more problem focus areas and teams of 3-5 build a comprehensive solution in five hours using the Global/Local Community Solutions Lab and its AI colleagues’ research assistance.

Participants do not need to code, and they are not judged on prototype polish. The design-science test is whether they accurately describe the problem, envision a compelling future, and comprehensively design a solution that can actually be deployed.

Each team gets access to the Global/Local Community Solutions Lab platform and AI Bucky, our AI co-pilot. After five hours, teams submit their solutions. Prizes can be awarded on a closing livestream an hour—or more, depending on the number of teams—later.

Design science is the organized use of imagination and science to develop innovative and viable solutions to critical real-world problems.

§ 02 · The community

Built for every community.

The host sets the problem focus and brings together people with different lived experiences, expertise, and perspectives. No prior design or technical experience is required.

Host-defined

Team-powered

Mobilize the people already in your community.

Teams of 3-5 combine local knowledge, practical experience, imagination, and AI-supported research.

Outcome-focused

Finish with a solution and implementation brief.

The result is a practical foundation for moving forward with your community-built solution, including funding proposals, partnerships, recruitment, and public outreach.

§ 08 · Cost

$1,000 to $2,000

The cost includes full use of the Global/Local Community Solutions Lab platform before and after the Solution-a-thon.

It also includes the Lab’s supporting tools for research, solution launch, post-Solution-a-thon strategy development, AI assistance, and publication of results in a variety of forms—from report, article, summary, and proposal draft to op-ed and even images of the solution and its implementation. Cash prizes, if your organization chooses to award them, are additional.
