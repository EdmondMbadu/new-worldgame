The premise

Not a hackathon. A solution sprint.

A traditional hackathon assumes the output is software. The Solution-a-thon keeps the urgency, teamwork, pitch energy, and prize money, but changes the deliverable.

Teams produce a Solution Brief: a structured, decision-ready design document showing the problem, preferred future state, intervention architecture, stakeholders, resources, first-year plan, second-order effects, and measurement plan.

The goal is not a compressed ten-day Global Solutions Lab. It is a different artifact built for a one-day sprint.

Proven playbook

New event. Tested methodology.

The Solution-a-thon is new, but the design-science method behind it comes from years of Global Solutions Lab work across the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

The 2026 sprint turns that longer methodology into a focused entry point: students leave with broad-brush solution strategies, schools bring more learners onto NewWorld Game, and sponsors can back visible, constructive work on real problems.
