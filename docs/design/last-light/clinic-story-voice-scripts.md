# Opening and closing voice scripts

Draft authored game narration for the campaign page's first five clinics. **Not verified clinician testimony.** No real doctor's identity is assigned. Durations below are audition targets; measure the actual recordings and create caption timestamps from the chosen takes. Actual clinic staff/actors need consent and field review. A synthetic take must remain disclosed as fictional AI narration.

## Delivery direction

Warm, calm, conversational, grounded in the clinic team's work. Natural breathing and short pauses, no radio filter, rushed dispatch cadence, pity appeal or exaggerated accent. The player brings equipment; the team continues caring. Use the same speaker for a clinic's opening and closing. The opening card contains factual context and labels fiction; game route/weather/deadline are not claimed to be real conditions.

## 01 — Ndingi Clinic

Context: already powered in the real campaign; this chapter is a re-created delivery. The documented real project is 2.0 kW and serves five named care areas. No population number is verified here.

Opening, approximately 25–30 seconds:

> Care does not stop when the sun goes down. Welcome to Ndingi. This journey re-creates a solar delivery inspired by a completed project. In the story, bring the battery and panels safely to our team. Follow the marked road, protect the kit, and stop in the receiving courtyard. You bring the power. We will keep caring.

Closing, approximately 15–20 seconds:

> The kit has reached our team. Your delivery is complete in this journey. In the real Ndingi project, solar power supports maternity, the laboratory, inpatient rooms, consultation and the operating room. Thank you for bringing the kit safely. We will keep caring.

## 02 — Centre de santé ophtalmologique CEAC / Nganga–Tsanga

Context: page places the clinic 17 km from Tsanga Nord and records it as visited/ready for final solar sizing. Do not infer a current blackout or specific medical equipment list.

Opening, approximately 20–25 seconds:

> Welcome to Nganga–Tsanga. Our clinic has been visited and documented for its next solar project. In this fictional delivery, you are bringing a kit to the receiving team. Take the marked route, give other vehicles room, and keep the battery and panels secure. We will be ready when you arrive.

Closing, approximately 12–16 seconds:

> You brought the kit safely to the team. This chapter is complete. Outside the game, the clinic's solar project still needs its final system size and installation schedule confirmed. Thank you for making the journey with us.

## 03 — Centre de santé ophtalmologique CEAC Nsioni

Context: visited/photographed; exact location, equipment needs and population are not provided by the inspected page.

Opening, approximately 20–25 seconds:

> Welcome to Nsioni. Reliable power is part of the clinic's next planned solar project. Tonight's route belongs to the game, and your task is simple: bring the kit safely to our team. Watch the road markers, wait when the crossing is occupied, and stop in the courtyard before the handover.

Closing, approximately 12–16 seconds:

> The journey is complete, and the kit is with our receiving team. Thank you for your careful delivery. The real clinic has been documented; its final solar system and installation schedule are still to be confirmed. The next chapter is waiting when you are ready.

## 04 — Centre de santé CEAC Kiobo–Kwimba

Context: exterior/treatment rooms/current condition photographed; specific service/equipment requirements are not established.

Opening, approximately 20–25 seconds:

> Welcome to Kiobo–Kwimba. Our buildings and treatment rooms have been documented for the solar project ahead. In this story, your delivery carries the kit to the clinic team. Keep a steady pace, read the road signs, and choose the firm route when you need it. We will receive you in the courtyard.

Closing, approximately 12–16 seconds:

## 05 — Centre Hospitalier Mont Sinaï

Context: Boma, Kongo Central; rooms/equipment to be powered must be confirmed. Do not assume the maternity/operating-room list from Ndingi applies here.

Opening, approximately 20–25 seconds:

> Welcome to Mont Sinaï, in Boma. The hospital has been visited as the team plans its solar project. This is your final delivery in the story. Bring the kit safely through the marked route, protect the panels and battery, and hand them over after stopping in the receiving courtyard. We will take it from there.

Closing, approximately 15–20 seconds:

> The final kit has reached the team. Five journeys are complete in this story. Thank you for driving with care. Real installations and their results are tracked separately by the project team. You brought the kit; the clinic teams carry the work forward.

## Optional approved population wording

Use only after the facility ID, figure, unit, source and assessment date have been matched and approved. Do not substitute the form cohort's values into the selected page cohort.

On-screen:

> Reported community served: approximately [X] residents.

Voice sentence for a closing:

> This clinic reports serving a community of around [X] residents. In this story, your kit has reached its team.

For Tabernacle's form, if that clinic is deliberately selected and approved:

> The clinic reports serving around four hundred households.

Never change that to “four hundred people,” multiply it by an assumed household size, or say that the player saved those lives. No catchment sum or repeated reach award on replay.

## Production notes

- These scripts intentionally contain provenance context while testimonies are unapproved. If a consenting real clinic representative supplies an approved testimony, keep their meaning and distinguish it from game instructions rather than putting invented words in their mouth.
- A future polished edit can put some simulation/installation-status context in the persistent card/transcript, but it must not disappear from the experience in a way that misrepresents the real clinic.
- Full controls/route overview are a readable opening drawer. Spoken messages stay short and never mention fixed keyboard letters that conflict with saved bindings.
- Start delivery, Next clinic and Home cancel the clip immediately; none can leak its last sentence into driving. Replay is explicit. A missing voice file is handled with the same transcript and no forced browser-speech fallback.
- Story captions follow the measured selected clip, and voice/captions have independent controls. There is no paid API call or generation delay during play.
